\section{Architecture and reproducible research}

\subsection{Representations and contracts}
Immutable packed and sparse binary values provide an inspectable exact
backend. Validated sparse CSS presentations expose syndromes and locality
without triggering rank computation. General elimination can densify;
structured ring methods therefore constitute separate capabilities with
explicit hypotheses and resource bounds.

Construction records retain ring seeds, action/physical-order conventions,
and canonical presentation fingerprints. Cyclic family manifests retain
Laurent templates independently of lift order and state the allowed
parameter domain. Monomial collisions under specialization can only
reduce incidence counts, giving uniform locality bounds from the template.
This proves neither positive rate nor distance growth. The implemented
manifest types cover specific constructions; they do not yet form a
general-purpose optimizing construction language.

CSS transformations and chain maps expose induced homology actions.
Quasi-isomorphism preserves homology but carries no automatic physical
weight guarantee. Future rewriting rules must distinguish algebraic
identities, metric preservation, and sparse measurement presentation.
No blanket zero-cost claim is made: validation, expansion, factorization,
and backend conversion have visible costs.

\subsection{Resumable bounded studies}
The SQLite study engine explores fixed-term scalar cyclic lifted-product
seeds using deterministic random sampling or greedy support mutations.
Trial-indexed randomness, exact configuration checks, source/runtime
provenance, and transactional evaluation records support reproducible
resumption. Cached records are reused only under the same construction
and evaluation semantics. Presentation hashes do not remove equivalent
codes described by different coordinates or seeds.

Eligibility requires available exact parameters and usable distance
analysis with protected logicals. The recorded Pareto front maximizes
logical dimension and conservative distance lower bounds while minimizing
check weight and qubit incidence degree. Upper-bound witnesses never
substitute for achieved distance. Zero-logical codes and skipped analyses
are distinguished explicitly. This is a restricted experimental baseline,
not evidence that an output code is novel or optimal.

Large campaigns require more than restarting a script. The current
transaction encompasses evaluation and serializes writers; frontier
construction is quadratic in distinct eligible evaluations. Those costs,
distance-search budgets, and algebraic work limits must be reported
alongside the scientific objective.

\subsection{Research priorities}
The next substantive improvements are stronger distance evidence with
independent verification, controlled decoder comparisons, and extraction
gadgets whose fault propagation and hardware constraints are evaluated
against the initial circuit baseline. Family search
should exploit mathematical structure or validated bounds, and compare
proposal strategies at equal budgets on held-out parameters.

Prime-power qudits, noncommutative modules, bosonic analytic operators,
and dynamical protocols remain distinct extensions. A native compiled
kernel or GPU backend should follow a demonstrated bottleneck. A compiler
framework becomes useful when transformation contracts and workloads
justify it; it does not remove combinatorial hardness or replace a
family-level mathematical proof.
